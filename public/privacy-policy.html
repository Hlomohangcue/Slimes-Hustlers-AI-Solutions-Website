Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Slimes.Hustlers AI Solutions ("Slimes.Hustlers", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal information when you visit slimeshustlers.com, contact us, or interact with our services.

Please read this policy carefully. By using our website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read it. Where consent is legally required, we will seek it separately.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact information: Your full name, email address, phone number, company name, and the message you submit through our website contact form.
  • Submission information: The date and time a contact submission is received, recorded by our website system.
  • Technical information: Information that may be processed by our hosting provider when you access the website, such as IP address, browser information, request details, and security or diagnostic logs.
  • Website preferences: Your light or dark theme preference, which our website stores in your browser's local storage.
  • Communications: Information you choose to provide when contacting us through email, WhatsApp, or other communication channels.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through the general website contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and consultation requests.
  • Understand your business requirements and discuss our services.
  • Communicate with you about a request or potential business relationship.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve our website.
  • Protect our website, detect misuse, and investigate security incidents.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

We do not state that submitting a contact form automatically subscribes you to marketing communications. Where required, we will obtain any additional consent needed for marketing.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing personal information, we may rely on your consent, steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests such as responding to business enquiries and securing our services. The applicable basis depends on the circumstances and the law that applies.

4. How Information Is Stored

Our website contact form sends submissions to our website backend. The backend sanitizes submitted fields, validates certain information, and attempts to store submissions in a Cloudflare Workers KV data store. The website also records a submission timestamp.

Our website is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process technical information as necessary to deliver, secure, and operate the website under its applicable service terms and privacy documentation.

We take steps intended to limit access to administrative contact records. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, managing business relationships, meeting legal obligations, and resolving disputes.

Actual retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances. We do not currently specify a fixed retention period in this policy. Information that is no longer needed should be deleted or otherwise disposed of where appropriate and technically feasible, subject to applicable legal obligations.
